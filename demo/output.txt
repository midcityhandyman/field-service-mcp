field-service-mcp demo (in-process mock vendor API, invented data, fixed dates)
Request from the office: "Taylor Mockwell called. The water heater still is not fixed.
Get someone back out there Tuesday morning and send a quote for a new heating element."

== Step 1: What can this server do? ==
    [read ] server_info: Describe this server: which adapter is active, whether writes are enabled, and working hours.
    [read ] list_customers: List customers, optionally filtered by a free text search.
    [read ] get_customer: Fetch one customer by id.
    [read ] list_jobs: List jobs, newest first. Filter by customer and by status.
    [read ] get_job: Fetch one job by id.
    [read ] list_visits: List visits, earliest first. Filter by job, technician and an inclusive date range.
    [read ] list_quotes: List quotes, newest first. Filter by customer and by status.
    [read ] get_quote: Fetch one quote with its line items and total.
    [read ] list_technicians: List the technicians who can be assigned to visits.
    [read ] get_schedule: Show who is booked where: visits grouped by technician and day, with job and customer names.
    [read ] find_open_slots: Find free windows inside working hours that can fit a visit of the given length.
    [WRITE] create_customer: Create a customer. Search with list_customers first to avoid duplicates.
    [WRITE] create_job: Create a job (a piece of work) for an existing customer. It starts in status open.
    [WRITE] create_visit: Book a technician on a job for a time window. Fails if the technician is already booked then.
    [WRITE] create_quote: Create a draft quote for a customer. The total is computed by the field service system.
  > server_info()
